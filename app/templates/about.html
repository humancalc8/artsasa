About ARTSASA Nairobi · Kenya

A gallery with a point of view.

ARTSASA is an independent contemporary art gallery based in Nairobi, Kenya. We create space for artists, ideas and work made with intention.

ARTSASA GalleryNairobi, Kenya
Who we are

Making room for good work.

ARTSASA works with artists whose practices explore identity, material, place, memory and the changing world around us.

Our programme brings together painting, photography, sculpture, woodwork, mixed media and other contemporary practices.

We are interested in work that has something to say — work that rewards attention and gives people a reason to stop, look and think.

Our approach

Artists first. Work always.

We work closely with artists to create exhibitions that give their practices room to breathe.

Rather than following a single aesthetic, ARTSASA is interested in strong ideas, honest materials and work that rewards attention.
