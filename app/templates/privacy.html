Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information ARTSASA may collect, why we use it, and how we work to keep your information protected.

01. Information we collect

When you use ARTSASA, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us and information generated through your use of the website.

Information you provide

Depending on how you use the website, this may include your name, email address, telephone number, account information, artwork enquiries and other information you choose to provide when communicating with ARTSASA.

Shopping and saved works

ARTSASA may use session information to maintain your shopping cart and saved artworks while you browse the website.

Technical information

Our hosting infrastructure and website systems may automatically process technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, requested pages and general usage data.

02. How we use information

We use information collected through ARTSASA for legitimate gallery and website operations, including:

  • responding to artwork enquiries and requests;
  • maintaining your account where you choose to create one;
  • maintaining shopping cart and saved-work functionality;
  • processing and communicating about purchases or acquisitions where applicable;
  • improving the website, its content and user experience;
  • protecting the website against abuse, fraud and security threats; and
  • communicating with you when you have requested or consented to receive communications.

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation of ARTSASA unless permitted by law or where you have provided the appropriate permission.

03. Cookies & local storage

ARTSASA uses browser technologies such as cookies and local storage to help the website function correctly and remember certain preferences.

For example, your browser may store information associated with your cart, saved artworks and cookie-consent preference.

Some cookies may also be required for security features such as protecting forms and maintaining your session.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain browser technologies may affect some website functionality.

04. Information sharing

ARTSASA does not sell your personal information.

We may share information where reasonably necessary to operate the website or provide a service you have requested. This may include website hosting, infrastructure, email delivery, security and other technical service providers.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, court order, or where necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of ARTSASA, our users or others.

05. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information handled through ARTSASA against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no website, server or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should therefore avoid submitting unnecessary confidential information through ordinary website forms.

06. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information that ARTSASA holds about you.

These may include requesting access to information, asking for inaccurate information to be corrected, requesting deletion where legally applicable, or objecting to certain processing.

If you have a privacy-related request, contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

07. Children

ARTSASA is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes that require parental consent.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

08. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology or legal obligations.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.

09. Contact ARTSASA

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or how ARTSASA handles information, please contact us.
