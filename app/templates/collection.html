Buying artwork

Buying art should feel simple.

Found something you love? You do not need to know everything about the art market before buying a work. Start with the piece that speaks to you. Our team can then help with availability, dimensions, delivery, payment and any other questions before you make your decision.

01

Choose a work

Browse the collection and open any artwork you would like to know more about. Check the artist, medium, year and displayed price.

02

Ask us anything

Use the WhatsApp button on the artwork or contact ARTSASA directly. We can confirm availability, dimensions, framing, delivery and other details.

03

Confirm your order

Once you are happy with the artwork, we will confirm the final order details with you before payment is completed.

04

Payment & delivery

Complete payment using the payment method provided by ARTSASA. Your artwork can then be arranged for collection or delivery according to the agreed terms.

Not sure what to choose?

Tell us the kind of space you are decorating, your preferred size, colours or budget and we can help narrow down the collection.

Buying as a gift?

Let us know that the artwork is a gift. We can help you choose a suitable piece and discuss presentation and delivery options.

Need more information?

Artwork is personal. If you want more photographs, dimensions, installation information or artist details, ask us before purchasing.
