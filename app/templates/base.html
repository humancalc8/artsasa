How to pay

  1. Open M-Pesa and choose Lipa na M-Pesa, then Buy Goods and Services.
  2. Enter till number 973810 and the amount we confirmed. The name shown should read Catherines Art Nouveau.
  3. Send us the confirmation message so we can reserve the work and arrange collection or delivery.

You can also scan the QR code, or dial *126# (Lipa na Bonga via My OneApp). Payments to the till work from Safaricom M-Pesa, Airtel Money and T-Kash.
